### About me

My research is at the intersection of **AI and databases**.

Before the PhD I spent 10 years in industry, most recently at **PingCAP (TiDB)** on AI applications
(GraphRAG, agentic app building) and the TiDB developer ecosystem.

  MySQL Connector/J, Hibernate, DBeaver and more; created the TiDB developer docs and their CI-tested examples.

  with production peaks above 10M QPS across the cluster.
